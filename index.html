Privacy Policy – Bill Kitab (بیل کتاب)

Developer: Etimad Software (اعتماد سافټویر), Afghanistan
Package: com.etimad.electronicshopreceipts
Last updated: 10 October 2026

Summary

Bill Kitab is an offline receipt book for shops. We, the developer, never receive, sell or share any of your data. The app has no account, no ads and no analytics. It uses the internet only for the optional Google Drive backup described below.

Data stored on your phone

Receipts, items, prices, customer names and phone numbers, payments, products and shop settings that you type into the app are saved only in a database on your own phone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose a Google account in the backup screen, the app saves backup files (your bills, products, customers, suppliers and settings) in a folder called "Bill Kitab Backups" in your own Google Drive. The app can only see files it created there. The files are sent over an encrypted connection directly to Google; they never pass through us. You can delete them in Google Drive at any time, and "Disconnect" removes the app's access.

Camera

The camera is used only to scan barcodes, and only when you open the scanner. Images are processed on the phone and not saved or sent anywhere.

Automatic backup on the phone

Once a day the app saves a backup file on your phone (in Documents/Bill Kitab). It stays on your phone.

Home-screen shortcut

If you choose to, the app adds a shortcut named after your shop to your home screen. The name stays on your phone.

Bluetooth

The app uses the "Nearby devices" (Bluetooth) permission only to connect to a thermal receipt printer that you have already paired. It does not scan for other devices to find your location and does not request location permission.

Backup and sharing

When you create a backup, the app writes a file to the place you choose with the Android file picker. When you share a receipt or statement, the app hands an image to the app you pick (for example WhatsApp). These happen only when you tap the button, and the data goes only where you send it.

Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
